The problem

Active market participants face fragmented data, noisy indicators, weak risk visibility, and excessive dependence on unverified trading claims.

Option-chain data is dense, fast-moving, and difficult to interpret without structure.
Retail traders often see signals without evidence, confidence boundaries, or risk context.
AI tools explain markets poorly when they are not connected to disciplined data and validation workflows.

Product direction

BetawithGamma is designed as a research and analytics layer, not a black-box profit engine.

Option-chain intelligence

Analytics for option-chain behavior, volatility shifts, liquidity context, and derivatives-market structure.

AI finance analyzer

Explanation layer for market summaries, scenario interpretation, risk context, and financial learning support.

Risk-first dashboards

Interfaces that emphasize uncertainty, evidence quality, risk zones, and decision-support boundaries.

Infrastructure plan

  • Cloud Run or Compute services for backend APIs
  • Firebase Hosting or static hosting for frontend delivery
  • Cloud SQL or Firestore for application and analytics data
  • Secret Manager for secure API and credential management
  • Cloud Logging and Monitoring for reliability tracking

AI and analytics plan

  • Gemini / Vertex AI for market explanation and summarization layers
  • BigQuery for historical analytics and evaluation datasets
  • Model-evaluation workflows for evidence scoring
  • Audit logs for AI output review and safety boundaries
  • Latency and reliability measurement before production scaling
