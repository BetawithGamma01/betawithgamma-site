AI-first fintech analytics startup

Market intelligence for options, volatility, and risk interpretation.

BetawithGamma is building an AI-assisted analytics platform for Indian derivatives and options-market research. The product converts live market data, option-chain metrics, volatility signals, and risk context into explainable dashboards for research, education, and decision support.

Contact Founder View Product Direction

Product thesis

Traders and learners face noisy option-chain data, fragmented tools, and weak risk visibility. BetawithGamma focuses on turning raw market signals into structured, explainable analytics.

Option-chain intelligence

Tools for monitoring option-chain behavior, volatility movement, liquidity context, and derivatives-market structure.

AI finance analyzer

AI-assisted explanation layer for market context, scenario interpretation, risk summaries, and learning support.

Risk-first dashboards

Visual interfaces designed to highlight uncertainty, risk zones, evidence quality, and interpretation limits.

Planned cloud architecture

Google Cloud is intended for scalable deployment, AI integration, monitoring, secure configuration, and analytics infrastructure.

Infrastructure

  • Cloud Run / Compute services for backend APIs
  • Firebase or static hosting for frontend delivery
  • Cloud SQL / Firestore for structured product data
  • Secret Manager for secure API and credential handling

AI and analytics

  • Gemini / Vertex AI for explanation and summarization layers
  • BigQuery for historical analytics and evaluation datasets
  • Cloud Logging and Monitoring for reliability tracking
  • Future model-evaluation workflow for evidence scoring

Roadmap

The next build phase is focused on evidence, reliability, and cloud migration — not exaggerated trading claims.

Near-term milestones

Evidence gates

  • Document product scope and non-advisory boundaries

  • Measure reliability before production scaling
